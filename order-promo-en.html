The Bakery Turns One 🎉

Celebrating with birthday prices

Hi, I'm Barak, the hands behind every loaf at Kafri Artisan Breads. My bread journey started over two decades ago, inspired by the home bakers I met in New Zealand. Over the years it grew into a real craft, and over the last decade I've focused on natural sourdough. I learned my craft hands-on, working in bakeries, and more recently through an advanced baking course at the San Francisco Baking Institute. All these years I've baked for family, friends, and neighbors — through good times and hard ones — and this past year, I've been happy to bake for this wonderful community here in Bergen County.

Every loaf I bake starts with natural levain and organic flour, stone-milled in a way that keeps the wheat's full nutrition and flavor intact — because I believe bread should be as good for you as it is delicious. Bread is a simple thing, and there's nothing I love more than knowing a loaf I baked made someone's table a little better today.

To celebrate our first year together, I wanted to say thank you the best way I know how — with fresh bread and bagels, at prices that let everyone come taste what my hands have baked. Welcome to the celebration!

— Barak, Kafri Artisan Breads
